Our Story

Educating Hearts & Minds in Yadgir

Located at Hosalli Cross, Yadgir, Rathnam English Medium High School has been the educational cornerstone of this district since 2006 — offering a state-board curriculum enriched with modern pedagogy, English-first communication, and holistic student development from Nursery all the way to Grade 10.

We believe every child is uniquely gifted. Our mission is to discover that gift, nurture it with care, and send students out as confident, compassionate, and capable citizens.
