We believe every child is uniquely gifted. Our mission is to discover that gift, nurture it with care, and send students out as confident, compassionate, and capable citizens.

Our Founder

Late Dr. M. Rathnam

Educationalist · Visionary · Inspiration

Born in the humble village of Naikal, Yadgir District, Dr. M. Rathnam's life is the ultimate testament to the transformative power of education. Rising from modest beginnings, he faced every obstacle with determination — studied hard, earned a Ph.D. in Education from the USA, and went on to become a Professor at Washington University.

"Quality education is not a privilege of the few — it is the birthright of every child, no matter where they come from."

He carried one great vision: to bring the quality of education he experienced at the world's finest institutions back to the children of Yadgir. In 2006, that vision became Rathnam School. He may no longer be with us, but his work remains forever.
